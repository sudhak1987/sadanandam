ABOUT US

Guided by Wisdom, Devoted to Service

Sadanandam Spiritual & Educational Trust nurtures character, knowledge and service, guided by the timeless wisdom and boundless compassion of Swami Sadanandam Ji Maharaj.

His Holiness Swami Sadanandam Ji Maharaj

A revered spiritual guide, teacher and humanitarian, Pujya Maharaj Ji has dedicated his life to awakening inner peace, guiding minds and uplifting society through selfless service. Under his guidance, the Trust was founded to carry this vision forward — building ashrams, schools and institutions that serve both the spirit and the community.

Today, the Trust's four pillars — Ashram, Gurukul, College and Institute — stand as living expressions of this mission, offering spiritual grounding, value-based education and skills for a lifetime of purposeful living.

Transform your spirit, Purify your mind,
and Discover a source of light, love and wisdom for the world.

Our Mission

To nurture character, knowledge and service, making timeless spiritual wisdom accessible to every seeker.

Our Vision

A world where inner peace and selfless service walk hand in hand with education and community upliftment.

Our Values

Compassion, integrity and service guide every ashram, school and program the Trust undertakes.
